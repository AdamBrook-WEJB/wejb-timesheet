2 Hours worked

Put your hours against the job you were on. If you've been off sick, leave those days blank and ring the office — sick pay is worked out separately. If you worked two jobs in a day, tap + another job to split the day. Leave a day blank if you weren't in.

3 Price work

Only if you're on price. Enter what you finished this week — the plot or area, what it was, and how many.

4 Variation work
